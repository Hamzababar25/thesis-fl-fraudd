Sign-Flip Attack Impact
Recall dropped from 54.2% (Round 0) to 13.6% by Round 20 under sign-flip with no defense. Multi-Krum + DP defense restored recall to 53.1% — matching the baseline.
Label-Flip Attack — New Finding
Data poisoning caused recall to collapse to 0.3% at Round 5. Multi-Krum provides partial recovery (~9.4% recall at Round 20). Gradient-level defenses are less effective against data-level poisoning — thesis contribution.

Thesis Claim Summary

What is Proven
FL framework correctly detects and rejects malicious clients under model poisoning (sign-flip, scale). Performance degrades under attack and recovers with Multi-Krum + clipping + DP across all 7 evaluated scenarios.
Limitations / Future Work
Label-flip (data poisoning) partially resists gradient-level defense. Dataset has low feature informativeness (ROC-AUC ≈ 0.50). More clients (5–10) and SMOTE-based balancing are future directions.
